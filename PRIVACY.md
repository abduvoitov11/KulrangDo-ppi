# Privacy Policy for Kulrang Doppi

**Last updated:** September 9, 2026  
**Extension Name:** Kulrang Doppi — Telegram Media Downloader  

Welcome to **Kulrang Doppi**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our browser extension handles user information in compliance with Google Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons developer policies.

---

### 1. Single Purpose and Core Functionality
Kulrang Doppi is designed solely to enable users to download media files (videos, photos, voice notes, and documents) from the Telegram Web application directly to their local computer.

---

### 2. Information Handled by the Extension
The extension only processes minimal technical data necessary to provide its features:

* **Local Preferences:** Your filter preferences (such as enabling/disabling image or video downloads) and download history are stored strictly locally on your device via the browser's `chrome.storage.local` API.
* **Downloads:** Media files selected by the user are downloaded directly to your local computer's default "Downloads" directory via the `chrome.downloads` API.
* **Network & Diagnostics:** Lightweight connectivity diagnostics are used solely to establish reliable connections to Telegram content delivery networks (CDNs).

---

### 3. What We DO NOT Collect
* We **do not** collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).
* We **do not** collect or view your Telegram account credentials, passwords, or authentication keys.
* We **do not** read, log, or store your private chats, contacts, or personal text messages.
* We **do not** use cookies or third-party tracking/advertising trackers.

---

### 4. No Remote Code Execution
Kulrang Doppi strictly adheres to Manifest V3 security requirements. The extension does not load, execute, or interpret any remotely hosted JavaScript code. All logic is packaged locally within the verified extension files.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services
The extension interacts only with official Telegram infrastructure (`*.telegram.org` and `api.telegram.org`) to fetch and download user-requested media files.

---

### 6. Data Security
Because all media processing occurs locally within your browser sandbox, your data remains strictly under your control on your personal machine.

---

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page.
